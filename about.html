Our Story

Founded in 2018, Luxora Decor began with a simple belief: every celebration deserves to be extraordinary. What started as a small team of passionate designers in Kerala has grown into a trusted name for wedding & event decoration across India.

We blend tradition with modern aesthetics, using premium materials, floral artistry, and immersive lighting to create stages that tell your unique love story. From intimate engagements to grand wedding receptions, we pour our heart into every detail.

We don't just decorate – we curate emotions.
